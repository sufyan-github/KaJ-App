# KAJ Competitor Study

**Task:** P0-RES-01
**Status:** draft — owner review required
**Research date:** 2026-08-18
**Scope:** Competitor facts only; the capability gap matrix is P0-RES-02.

## Method and evidence rules

This is public-web desk research for KAJ's Rajshahi-first marketplace. It covers every competitor and informal substitute required by `KAJ_BUILD_GUIDE.md` Part C.1. Public websites, help centres, app-store listings, company pages, and clearly identified directory pages were reviewed. No private account, paid plan, installed app, interview, or live transaction was used.

- `H` means a current first-party page directly supports the finding.
- `M` means a first-party marketing/help page supports it but important operational detail is absent.
- `L` means the source is third-party, old, ambiguous, or the finding is an explicit inference.
- `Unknown` means the reviewed public sources did not disclose the fact. It is not evidence that the feature does not exist.
- `ASSUMPTION` identifies an inference to validate in later research.
- Platform claims such as “verified,” “safe,” user counts, or transaction counts are recorded as claims, not independently verified facts.
- No unpublished commission, take rate, or fee is inferred. Historical prices are labelled and are not treated as current.

## Evidence limitations and next research handoff

This study compares observable public mechanisms; it does not validate product legitimacy, current market liquidity, service quality, legal compliance, or claimed verification. Several local sources expose little more than marketing or directory information. P0-RES-03 must investigate Rajshahi channel usage, category frequency, neighbourhood coverage, phone/data constraints, payment habits, and broker/tutor practices. P0-RES-04 must then test these findings in interviews and concierge transactions.

P0-RES-02 will turn this evidence into the prescribed capability gap matrix. It must keep facts distinct from KAJ design choices and must not treat a competitor's feature as proof of local desirability.
